# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS (native; not one of web/ios/android/adaptive, so no mobile references apply)

## Stack

SwiftUI + AppKit panels, Swift Package, macOS 13+. Confirmed by user.

## Users

Anyone who sits at a desk all day (confirmed). Sees the app mid-work, usually in focus; the app must interrupt gently and be easy to dismiss.

## Product Purpose

Gazewell: a menu bar app that reminds the user to rest eyes, fix posture and blink, timed around natural pauses. Success: breaks actually get taken and the app is never annoying enough to be quit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Eye-break reminders with no iOS sync, Live Activities or App Store release. Heads-up before break (start now / +1 / +5 / +15), cursor-following countdown, smart auto-pause (calls, screen recording, video, fullscreen games, Focus), planned breaks, custom messages/backgrounds/sounds, global hotkeys, AppleScript/Shortcuts hooks, launch at login.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Gazewell. Calm, wellness tone; its own look (seven switchable themes), no third-party logos or assets.

## Evidence on Hand

No logos, screenshots or testimonials to reuse.

## Product Principles

- Never feel like an alarm: gentle in, easy out.
- The break itself is the hero: one number, one line of copy, nothing else.
- Quiet by default, configurable by depth.
- Respect context: never break in on calls, recordings or games.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Respect Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast; full keyboard control (Esc skips); VoiceOver labels on controls; countdown text readable at distance.
